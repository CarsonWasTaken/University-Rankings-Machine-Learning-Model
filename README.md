# University-Rankings-Machine-Learning-Model
Problem Statement: What makes a university “good”/how can analysis of factors help students in the college application/decision process?
Focus on the metrics of what makes universities ranked higher than others.
Analyze the global context behind higher education rankings.
Analyze trends and patterns in the data provided using data mining tools and machine learning.
Determine correlation between the different factors that contribute to the rankings.
Visualize our findings in a comprehensive way.
For this project, we used the “World University Rankings 2023” dataset from Kaggle.
Reveals the metrics of university rankings, including: Research Score, Female:Male Ratio, Students:Staff Ratio, Teaching Score, Industry Income Score, Number of Students, Location, International Outlook Score, and Overall Score.
We trained our dataset on decision tree to predict rankings based on the factors score.
https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/alitaqi000/world-university-rankings-2023
The existing dataset provides the various factors mentioned that contributes to the rankings.
We aim to analyze the data behind the factors to determine how much impact these individual factors have on the rankings.
By analyzing and comparing the factors, we can determine correlation and trends, as well as provide more insights into the universities in a global context.
Helping the millions of students applying to college every year.
